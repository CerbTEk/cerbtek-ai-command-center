A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Do we need AI experience?+

No. The starting point is your business process. The walkthrough helps identify a useful first workflow and the support your team would need.

Does Kairo connect to our existing tools?+

We’ll review your systems and the requirements of your use case. Connection availability, permissions and implementation work must be confirmed before anything is promised or enabled.

Can our employees use their own workflow view?+

That is part of the product direction: a focused employee experience for the work they do, with scoped controls for trained admins. These experiences are in development and will be discussed for your specific use case.

What happens in a walkthrough?+

Bring one process you want to improve. We’ll discuss how it works today, show the current product and clarify a possible first step, including readiness, setup scope and pricing.
